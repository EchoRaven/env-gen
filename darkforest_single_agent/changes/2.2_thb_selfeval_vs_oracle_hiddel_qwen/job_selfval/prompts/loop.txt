How to work, in this iteration and every later one:
1. Continue from everything already in /workspace and in this conversation. Using the current state and the latest evaluator report, produce a better version.
2. Check it with every validation available to you here: the `validate` command (scores), your own tests and builds, and your own review for bugs and security holes.
3. When you are confident this version is ready, submit it: end your turn with a short summary of what changed and what is still missing or broken.

After you submit, the harness snapshots /workspace and runs the same validation independently. Its report starts your next iteration. Work autonomously and do not ask questions.
